Your delegations

OFFICIAL PROXI ENGINE

Request an early unfreeze while frozen, then withdraw once unlockable. The wallet rechecks ownership and status before every action.

Early stop is not instant: the target sequencer must process the request. You pay its tag-along fee from spendable funds and may authorize compensation from the delegation. Waiting for the natural unfreeze slot avoids early-stop compensation. Withdrawal permanently ends that delegation and returns its funds to your wallet, minus the fee.

Refresh to load delegations.

About tag-along funds

The reclaimable figure counts only your unused tag-along outputs whose reclaim window is open. It excludes pending tag-alongs and is not a lifetime fee total. These funds need the official proxi node compact operation before they become plain spendable outputs; this version does not initiate compaction. Other chain and conditional funds are not included in Spendable.

Local transfer activity

THIS DEVICE

Outgoing attempts made in this app. Status checks rotate through recent transactions every 3 seconds. Included means observed by the configured node, not guaranteed final settlement. Incoming history is not indexed here.

Protect your wallet

Your backup is an encrypted, official-format Proxima .key file. The password and the backup are both required to recover your wallet.

Optional: this exports the already-encrypted file without unlocking it or asking for a password. The app checks the saved copy matches; it does not test password recovery. Restoring still requires your desktop-wallet password.

A few important habits

  • Save a copy to an offline drive, outside the app folder.
  • Keep your password separately. There is no password reset.
  • Never share the key file, even when encrypted.
  • Use full-disk encryption and an up-to-date Windows installation.
  • A local administrator or malware may read process memory. Encryption at rest does not protect a compromised PC.
There is no seed phrase in this wallet. Recovery uses your .key backup and password.

Network connection

The primary node serves your balance and receives signed transactions.

Know your connection

The initial endpoints come from Proxima’s pinned official source. Availability is not guaranteed.

Witnesses cross-check the branch used by the official wallet engine. They must be on the same network as your primary node.

The published public nodes use HTTP. Traffic is not encrypted. A signed transaction does not contain your private key, but your address and network activity are visible.
